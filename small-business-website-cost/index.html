What A Small Business Website Actually Costs Around Here.

We read the published pricing pages of Denver-area web design firms so you don't have to collect quotes to find out. Here is the short version: comparable small-business sites start at about $1,800 to $4,500 depending on the firm, and care plans commonly run around $100 a month. The long version, and a much cheaper way to get the same thing, is below.

01

The Upfront Build

Across the Denver-area firms that publish package pricing, comparable multi-page small-business builds start anywhere from about $1,800 for a basic five-page site up to $4,500 at the top of the published range. Bigger and more custom builds run higher from there.

02

The Monthly Care Plan

Most firms also charge a monthly plan for hosting, security, and maintenance. Published rates run from about $50 to $250 a month, with most clustering around $100. What's included varies, check whether content edits are in the plan or billed separately.

03

The Cost Nobody Lists

Your domain name is a separate, small cost, roughly $10–20 a year at a registrar. Whoever you hire, the domain should be registered in your name, on your account. If it's in the designer's name, your web address is their property, not yours.
