Questions

Before you ask.

Who owns the domain?

You do. If you already own a domain, we point it at the new site. If you don’t, we register one together — in your name, on your own registrar account, paid by you (roughly $10–20 a year). That’s deliberate: the domain is your property from minute one, and if you ever move the site, it goes with you.

How do edits work?

Just reply to any email from us with what you want changed — new photos, new hours, a new service. Up to 30 minutes of updates a month is included in the monthly. We confirm every change before it goes up, and most land within a couple of business days. One round of corrections at setup is included too.

What if I want to cancel?

Email us and we’ll stop the monthly billing. The monthly is what covers your hosting, security, upkeep, and edits, so those services end when it stops. Your domain stays registered in your name either way, and the site itself is yours to move — you’re never locked in to us to keep your web address.

How fast will my site be live?

Within 48 hours of your domain being pointed over. Two things sit on your side of the clock: your answers to the short edits form, and domain access. If either stalls, the clock pauses at that gate — and we’ll tell you the same day rather than let it slip quietly.

How does payment work?

The $50 signup is paid through Stripe. The $37.50 monthly is a Stripe subscription that starts when your site goes live — not before. Prefer to talk first? Book the call below and we’ll sort out payment together.
